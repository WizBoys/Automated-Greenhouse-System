Automated Greenhouse System

Introduction

The Automated Greenhouse System is an innovative approach to modern farming that integrates sensors, microcontrollers, and automation techniques to optimize plant growth. This system allows farmers to regulate environmental conditions efficiently, reducing labor costs and increasing productivity.

Components

  • Arduino UNO R3
  • Temperature and Humidity Sensor (DHT11/DHT22)
  • Soil Moisture Sensor
  • Ultrasonic Sensor
  • OLED Display

How It Works?

The system utilizes a microcontroller, such as the Arduino UNO R3, to process data from sensors monitoring temperature, humidity, soil moisture, and light intensity. Based on predefined thresholds, actuators like fans, heaters, and irrigation systems are controlled to maintain ideal growing conditions.

Advantages

  • Enhanced productivity and yield
  • Efficient resource management (water and energy conservation)
  • Reduced labor dependency
  • Scalability for small and large farming applications

Disadvantages

Despite its benefits, the Automated Greenhouse System has certain disadvantages:

  • High initial setup cost
  • Complex maintenance and technical knowledge required
  • Dependence on electricity and power backup
  • Possible sensor failures affecting accuracy

Implementation

  1. Design and layout planning
  2. Component selection and installation
  3. Programming and testing
  4. System calibration and operation

Challenges and Future Enhancements

Challenges such as high initial costs, sensor calibration, and power dependency can be addressed with cost-effective solutions and renewable energy integration. The future scope includes AI-based predictive analytics and IoT-enabled remote monitoring.
